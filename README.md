# BetaList

BetaList is a platform for discovering upcoming startups and beta products, connecting early adopters with founders seeking their first users and feedback. Founded in 2010, BetaList curates and features pre-launch and recently launched internet startups, giving founders exposure through its website, email newsletter, and social media channels. Startups can be submitted for free or pay for expedited review and featuring. Each startup may be featured twice — once pre-launch and once at launch — and gains access to an audience of engaged early adopters interested in discovering the next wave of technology products.

**URL:** [https://betalist.com/](https://betalist.com/)

## APIs

No official public developer API has been identified for BetaList. Startup discovery and submission are handled through the web interface. BetaList does provide a newsletter at [https://betalist.com/newsletter](https://betalist.com/newsletter) and submission guidelines at [https://betalist.com/criteria](https://betalist.com/criteria).
