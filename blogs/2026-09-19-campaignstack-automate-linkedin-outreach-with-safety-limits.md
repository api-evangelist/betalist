CampaignStack is campaign orchestration for B2B lead gen agencies. It runs LinkedIn and email outreach with per-account daily budgets that increase over weeks, a dedicated residential proxy per account, and a review queue where you approve AI-drafted messages before they send. Auto-send is opt-in.
