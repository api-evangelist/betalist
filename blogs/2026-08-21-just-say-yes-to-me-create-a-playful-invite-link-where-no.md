Just Say Yes to Me lets you send a playful invite link that makes No dodge and Yes easy to tap. Fill in names and a question, share the link in any messenger, and get their reply straight to your inbox without creating an account. You can add touches like confetti on Yes, themes for occasions, a photo, your song, and a secret note.
