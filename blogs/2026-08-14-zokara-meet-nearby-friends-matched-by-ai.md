ZOKARA helps you meet compatible people nearby by using AI to match personalities, interests, and lifestyle, then nudges you to connect through real-world hangouts and local activities. It focuses on friendships, not dating, so you can build a genuine social circle. Whether you’re new in town, starting college, or expanding your network, ZOKARA surfaces people you’ll vibe with and makes it easy to join hangouts and create lasting connections.
