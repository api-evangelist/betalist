Carom turns your team’s email into a shared, AI-powered source of truth for customer relationships. It automatically assembles contacts, organizations, emails, files, events, and tasks from the messages you already send, so everyone sees the full picture without manual data entry. Connect your inbox in seconds, set granular sharing rules, and let built-in agents summarize threads, tag activity, create and close tasks, and prepare briefings.
