Kong is a free online booking system for Malaysian tour and activity operators. It lets you accept bookings, process payments via Stripe or Xendit, and manage availability across shared resources like ATVs, boats, and guides. Set group pricing, deposits, and promo codes, build custom guest forms, and capture digital waivers.
