Everyone flatters your idea — friends, co-founders, even ChatGPT. Tribunal of Ideas runs a live courtroom-style evaluation instead: dozens of AI models search the web for real evidence and competitors, then stage prosecution, defense, witnesses, and a 12-model jury that scores nine criteria before a formula calculates the verdict. You get a replayable trial, a shareable verdict card, and a downloadable PDF report.
