Wondershare Relumi is an AI-powered mobile photo editor that lets you retake what you can't replay. Backed by Wondershare's 20+ years of digital experience, it instantly fixes imperfect portraits, restores faded memories, and animates still images into cinematic videos. Powered by the Nano Banana Pro algorithm, Relumi understands faces and textures instead of applying flat filters.
