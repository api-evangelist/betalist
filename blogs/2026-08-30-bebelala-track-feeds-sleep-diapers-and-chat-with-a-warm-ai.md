bebelala is a cozy baby tracker that helps caregivers manage feeding, sleep, diapers, medicine, and daily activities in one calm place. It offers one-tap timers for breast, bottle, and pumping, quick logs for diapers and sleep, a weather-aware dressing helper, and growth and milestone tracking. Built-in lullabies, stories, and ambient sounds play while you log.
