Groundnote is a meeting-to-execution platform that captures what was decided, who committed, and what happens next. It records meetings across Zoom, Meet, Teams, or uploads, then links every decision, task, and risk to source evidence with confidence labels and a full changelog. Teams verify commitments, resurface stale items, and sync work to Jira, Asana, Linear, Slack, and calendars.
