TickClip gives you a buyer-first verdict on any Amazon product. Paste a link or search, and it checks the price against 30- and 90-day averages, tests review authenticity, and scores seller reputation to flag manipulation. In seconds, you get one clear call—tick (buy now), clip (save and wait), or skip (walk away)—with no commissions or affiliate bias.
