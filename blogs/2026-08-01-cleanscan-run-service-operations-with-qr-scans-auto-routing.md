CleanScan is an operations platform for cleaning and facilities service teams that turns incident reports into routed work, then captures photo evidence, notes, and timestamps as proof of completion. It provides real-time visibility across zones and client sites, helps spot patterns, and standardizes reporting. Contractors and facility managers share one portal to resolve issues quickly and document work clients can trust.
