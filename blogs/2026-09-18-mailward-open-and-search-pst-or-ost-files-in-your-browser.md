Mailward lets you open, browse, search, and export Outlook PST and OST files directly in your browser. It also reads OLM (Outlook for Mac), MBOX (Google Takeout), EML, and MSG files. It parses archives locally with the File API and a Web Worker, keeping your data on your device and allowing multi-gigabyte files to load without exhausting memory.
