Y Build is a shared workspace where founders and product teams coordinate specialist AI agents on real product work. It brings planning, building, review, approvals, and launch readiness into one visible workflow instead of juggling disconnected AI chats. Teams keep context and deliverables together while humans remain in control of decisions and approvals.
