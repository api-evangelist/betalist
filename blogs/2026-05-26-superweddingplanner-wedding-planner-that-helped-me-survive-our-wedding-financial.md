SuperWeddingPlanner started with a simple problem: my wife wanted a luxury wedding, and I wanted to still own a home afterwards. The wedding industry often has opacity—vendor markups, vague quotes, and many spreadsheets that still miss things. Tell SuperWeddingPlanner about your wedding—guest count, style, budget, location—and in 30 seconds it drafts a personalized plan with a timeline, supplier categories, and a realistic budget that actually adds up. From there, you can track estimated vs. actual spend, manage your private supplier shortlist, handle RSVPs and dietary needs, and design seating layouts that fit your venue. View startup
