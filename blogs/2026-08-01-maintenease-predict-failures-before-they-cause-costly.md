MaintenEase provides maintenance and facility teams with a CMMS featuring built-in predictive maintenance. It monitors assets and calculates a failure-risk score from 0 to 100 every few hours, turning rising risk into scheduled work orders and keeping complete histories, readings, and costs in one place. You can manage work orders, preventive maintenance, assets, downtime, and KPIs, collaborate on mobile, and accept public requests through a branded portal.
