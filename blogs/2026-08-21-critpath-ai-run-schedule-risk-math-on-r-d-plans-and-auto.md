CritPath AI is a schedule-risk platform for R&D and industrial scale-up programs in biotech, deep tech, and federally funded projects. It runs CPM, CCPM/TOC, and Monte Carlo simulations on your live plan to show date confidence, highlight early risks, and auto-replan when vendors slip. Decision gates are audited objects, and AI proposes scoped changes that you approve.
