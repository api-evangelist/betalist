TimeTic is a digital time clock and attendance system for self-employed owners and small teams. Employees clock in via QR code, PIN, web app, or iOS and Android, with GPS-verified location, break tracking, and live attendance. You can export hours to PDF or CSV, correct mistakes with an audit log, and meet EU time-tracking requirements.
