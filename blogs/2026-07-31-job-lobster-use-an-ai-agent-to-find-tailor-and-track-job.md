JobLobster is an AI job search agent that finds better-fit roles, tailors your resume and cover letters in your voice, drafts follow-ups, and helps you get interview-ready. Upload your resume once and it scans ATS feeds, scores roles from 0 to 10, manages a shortlist, and prepares application packages for your review and approval. It remembers your stories, tracks your pipeline, and offers science-backed interview practice, with a free tier and a Pro plan at $39/month.
