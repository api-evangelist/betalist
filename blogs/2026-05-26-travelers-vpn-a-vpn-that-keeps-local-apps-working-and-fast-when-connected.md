Traveler's VPN is an iPhone, iPad, and Mac VPN client that keeps your destination's local apps working when you connect. Unlike other VPNs that reroute everything globally and break maps, ride-share, food delivery, local payment, and banking apps, Traveler's keeps them all working. Your local apps stay local and up to 10 times faster, while home apps still reach home. The app costs $2.99 once, supports iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and requires no subscription or account.
