Covington Dispatch lets trades and field service pros create jobs in seconds, add line items, labor, parts, photos, and send invoices by text so customers can tap and pay. It runs in your phone’s browser with big buttons and high contrast for work in the field, and you can add it to your home screen—no app store needed. You keep what you earn with a flat monthly subscription while payments process via Stripe. Start fast with zero training and scale from solo operators to crews with simple, honest pricing.
