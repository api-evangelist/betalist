Orelon is an AI video generator that lets creators produce cinematic-quality videos from simple text prompts or images. It makes complex visual effects and camera movements easy to use through intuitive AI tools. The platform focuses on creative control and efficiency, letting users quickly prototype and change video concepts without needing technical skills.
