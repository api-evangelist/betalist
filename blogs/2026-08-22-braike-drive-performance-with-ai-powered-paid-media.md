Braike is a digital marketing agency that combines expert paid media management with a suite of AI tools. It supports organizations of any size through ongoing budget management or one-off missions to boost performance across channels. Its Braike Suite includes CheckUp, which finds account issues, Pilot, which applies fixes at scale, and Sentinel, which monitors around the clock.
