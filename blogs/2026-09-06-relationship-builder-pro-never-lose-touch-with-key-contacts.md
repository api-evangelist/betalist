Relationship Builder Pro is a personal CRM that helps busy professionals stay connected with the people who matter. Import contacts from your phone or LinkedIn, set connection goals, and get timely nudges, AI conversation starters, and scheduled messages to ensure follow-ups never slip. Use health scores to see who needs attention today, automate thoughtful check-ins around milestones like birthdays, and centralize customers, champions, and mentors in one place.
