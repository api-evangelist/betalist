StreamingBeam solves the common frustration of not knowing which service has what you want to watch. Search any movie or TV show and instantly see every platform that carries it across Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, Max, Paramount+, Apple TV+, Peacock, and Amazon Prime Video. Features include AI-powered Tonight's Pick recommendations based on your mood, Leaving Soon alerts so you never miss expiring content, a Streaming Cost Calculator to check if you're paying for the right services, and Ask the Beam, an AI chat assistant for personalized suggestions.
