PicLumen is an all-in-one creative platform for AI image and video generation with a built-in community. You can pick text-to-image, image-to-image, text-to-video, or image-to-video, then add effects, templates, and editing tools to produce polished content. Create with daily free Lumens or upgrade for more, download your results, and share them in Challenges, Explore, and the Hub.
