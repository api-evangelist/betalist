Logi Tot learns your baby's unique rhythm to forecast naps, feeds, and diaper changes, turning quick logs into personalized foresight. You can track sleep, feeding, diapers, growth, and milestones in one place and see live predictions from day one to age seven. It supports voice logging, milk inventory, shared caregiving, and clean checkup reports, with guidance based on WHO and CDC frameworks.
