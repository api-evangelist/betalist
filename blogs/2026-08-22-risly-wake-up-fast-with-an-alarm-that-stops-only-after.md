Risly is an anti-snooze alarm for iPhone that only turns off after you complete a quick mission. Pick a wake-up time and choose what it takes to stop the alarm: scan a toothbrush or barcode, solve math problems, shake the phone, do push-ups, or scan a QR code. When it rings, it bypasses Focus, Do Not Disturb, and silent mode, with no snooze and no loopholes.
