Trustity unifies endpoint and cloud security in one portal and agent. The TAO agent enforces policies on devices and browsers, powering VisionX visual DLP to detect cameras aimed at screens, GenGuard to block sensitive pastes into GenAI and shadow IT, Vault & PAM for zero-knowledge secrets and credential rotation, and HostGuard IPS to stop brute-force attacks. Teams manage policies, agents, and live events in the Trustity Cloud Portal to reduce console sprawl and protect data on endpoints.
