SkinAI is an AI skin health app that helps you scan, analyze, and track skin conditions over time. With one photo, SkinAI uses AI technology to identify concerns such as acne, eczema, rash, psoriasis, dermatitis, fungal infections, and suspicious moles. Built with European dermatology expertise, SkinAI provides fast, clinically informed assessments.
