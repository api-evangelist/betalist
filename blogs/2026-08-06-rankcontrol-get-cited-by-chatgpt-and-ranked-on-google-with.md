RankControl helps brands win AI search by creating and publishing long-form, citation-optimized content that chatbots cite and Google ranks. Seven AI agents find buyer questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and Google, write brand-matched guides, and publish them as native posts on your domain with your approval. It tracks citations, backlinks, and leads by source, automates outreach, and integrates with WordPress, HubSpot, Webflow, Slack, and your CRM.
