AIFlightOps is an operational platform to manage professional drone jobs from start to finish. It lets you design missions on a map, define phases, parameters, and deliverables, and runs a regulatory analysis in under 2 minutes using official AESA and ENAIRE sources, producing briefings and downloadable documents. It connects companies with certified UAS operators who receive missions and submit proposals with price and equipment.
