Certyneo combines e-signature, a secure document vault, and AI-powered contract analysis in one platform. E-signatures hold up legally with a full audit trail meeting eIDAS/AES standards in the EU, and QES-level signatures for the strongest legal weight. The AI reviews contracts before you do, flagging risky clauses and unusual language, and can draft a first contract from a plain-language brief.
