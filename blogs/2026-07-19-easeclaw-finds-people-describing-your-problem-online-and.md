EaseClaw automates lead generation by monitoring 12 platforms for people describing problems you solve, scoring each post 0–100 for buying intent, and drafting ready-to-send replies in your voice. You review, tweak, and send them. It learns from your website, filters to your ideal customers, and delivers ranked leads via digest and alerts.
