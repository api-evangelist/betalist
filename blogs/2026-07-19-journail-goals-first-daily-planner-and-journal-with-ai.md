Journail is a daily planner and journal built around your goals instead of an endless to-do list. Each morning, it creates a realistic plan from your tasks, calendar, missed items, and a few new steps from your goals — so you spend the day on what matters, not triaging five open tabs. Through the day, you capture anything by typing to the built-in assistant, which turns it into a task, note, or event.
