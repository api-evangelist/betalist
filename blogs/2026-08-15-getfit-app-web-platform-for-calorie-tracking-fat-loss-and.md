GetFIT App is a web-based fitness membership that gives you personalized calorie and macro targets, food logging, high-protein recipes, 4-week meal plans, and structured workout programs. You can use it on any device to track weight, measurements, habits, and intermittent fasting. It helps you calculate your goals, follow step-by-step plans, and build consistent habits for fat loss and muscle gain.
