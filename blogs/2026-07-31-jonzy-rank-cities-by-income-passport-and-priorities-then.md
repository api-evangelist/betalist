Jonzy helps you decide where to live by ranking 356 cities based on your income, passport, and priorities. Build a profile once, and it recalculates costs, quality-of-life scores, and visa-friendly options tailored to you. It checks over 100 long-stay and digital nomad programs for eligibility, offers head-to-head city comparisons, and includes a move planner with timelines, budgets, and checklists.
