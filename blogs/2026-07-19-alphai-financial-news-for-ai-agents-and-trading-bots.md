alphai delivers market-moving news structured for trading automations. It scans over 10,000 articles from more than 6,900 sources daily, deduplicates them, and assigns a relevance score from 1 to 10 with per-ticker tags and categories. SEC filings flow from EDGAR to an enriched, ticker-tagged feed in about six minutes.
