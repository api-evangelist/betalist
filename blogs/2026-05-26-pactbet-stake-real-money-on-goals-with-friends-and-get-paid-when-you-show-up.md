PactBet lets you stake real money on goals with friends and get paid when you show up. Create a pact, set a check-in time, and invite your crew to match your stake. At check-in, everyone submits a fresh live photo and the group votes to verify. Pass the vote and keep your stake; miss it and forfeit. Those who show split the pot from those who don't, minus a 5% fee. Use it for workouts, study sessions, sprints, or anything else you can verify with a photo, then cash out instantly. View startup
