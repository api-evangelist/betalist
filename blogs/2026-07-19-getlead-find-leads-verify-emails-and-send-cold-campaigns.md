Getlead combines lead scraping, email verification, cold email sending, and a built-in CRM into one workflow. It pulls prospects from LinkedIn, the web, and a 420M+ contact database, verifies addresses with SMTP, and launches AI-personalized sequences across multiple inboxes to drive replies. Track every open and reply in a unified inbox, manage pipelines, and sync contacts to tools like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zapier.
