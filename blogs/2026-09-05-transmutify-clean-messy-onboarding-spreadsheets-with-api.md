Transmutify streamlines customer onboarding by turning messy files into clean, import-ready records. It collects uploads through your product while its API handles schema mapping, validation, and a hosted human review before delivering approved JSON or CSV back to your workflow. It supports CSV, TSV, XLSX, TXT, and images, safeguards PII during AI mapping, and verifies webhooks.
