ScrollToll is a digital wellness app that turns mindless scrolling into a fitness trade. Instead of soft prompts, it introduces "positive friction" by locking distracting apps behind movement hurdles. Using on-device AI camera tracking, it verifies exercises like squats or pushups in real-time, converting your reps into screen-time minutes you can bank and spend.
