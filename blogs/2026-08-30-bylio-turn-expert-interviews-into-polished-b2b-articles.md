Bylio helps B2B marketing teams turn expert knowledge into publish-ready content. It guides your subject-matter expert through a short, adaptive interview, spoken or typed, then drafts articles, blog posts, or case studies in your brand voice based solely on what they said. The workflow tracks stages, sends reminders, and shows what needs attention while your team reviews and approves before anything goes live.
