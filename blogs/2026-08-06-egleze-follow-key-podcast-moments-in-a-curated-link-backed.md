Egleze is a newsroom for podcasts that surfaces the day's most important moments from independent shows. It curates short, sourced clips into a live broadcast, daily brief, and topic pages so you can follow key topics and dive deep. Editors track over 100 podcasts, index themes, and link every moment back to its source.
