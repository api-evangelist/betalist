GreenGo is a Windows desktop app (MacOs comming soon) that automates the entire DJ library preparation pipeline. Paste a URL from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, SoundCloud, or 6 other platforms - GreenGo downloads the audio, detects BPM and musical key, analyzes energy and mood, converts the format, and writes everything directly into the file's ID3 metadata. Open Rekordbox, Serato, Traktor, or VirtualDJ - your tracks are already tagged and ready to play. View startup
