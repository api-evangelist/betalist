Shareof.ai tracks how AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI, and more recommend brands across buyer prompts. It shows where you appear, who wins instead, and which sources shape answers so you can target citations, content, and outreach. The platform monitors AI crawler visits, offers privacy-first web analytics, and compares visibility by location.
