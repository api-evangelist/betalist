MixTranslate is an AI translation tool that runs your text through 20+ AI models such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and others, displaying the results side by side. Instead of guessing which model will handle your content best, you paste your text once, pick your languages, and compare the outputs in one place. It supports 150+ languages, from short messages to longer documents, and provides quality scores so you can see which version reads most naturally before you copy it.
