FortripAI is travel decision infrastructure for complex trips. It helps travelers turn conversations into structured multi-city itineraries, then validate, optimize, and book them with real-world constraints in mind. FortripAI compares city sequences, transportation options, hotel combinations, transfer times, travel dates, and total trip cost while flagging hidden risks before booking.
