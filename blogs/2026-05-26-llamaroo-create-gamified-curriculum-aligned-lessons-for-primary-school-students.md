Llamaroo is an AI lesson platform for primary school teachers and parents that generates gamified, standards-aligned activities in minutes. It helps teachers plan engaging lessons that students actually want to play, aligned to UK and US programs like Eureka Math, enVision, Into Reading, and Amplify CKLA. Use Llamaroo to quickly build interactive lessons for any topic including math, reading, science, and more, then share them with your class. The platform supports parents, teachers, and schools and follows strict privacy practices to avoid tracking children or exposing them to an AI model. View startup
