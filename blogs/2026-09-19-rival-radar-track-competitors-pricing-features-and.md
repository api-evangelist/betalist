Rival Radar monitors competitors’ websites for meaningful changes and explains them in plain English. It tracks pricing pages, feature lists, messaging, changelogs, careers, integrations, and more, filtering out noise like cookie banners. You add competitors or describe your business and let AI find them, then receive prioritized alerts and before-and-after diffs.
