VendPlacer is a marketplace that connects vending machine operators with premium property locations worldwide. Operators can browse over 19,580 verified spaces across 310 cities, filter by type, foot traffic, and budget, then contact owners to negotiate placement and confirm machine fit. A dashboard helps track inquiries and placements.
