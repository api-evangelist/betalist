Concourse is the AI execution layer for enterprise finance. It builds and runs agents that adapt to your data, logic, and workflows to handle variance analysis, close, forecasting, accounts receivable, and weekly reviews. Outputs are audit-ready with full traceability and governance, delivered via email, Slack, Teams, Excel, and PowerPoint.
