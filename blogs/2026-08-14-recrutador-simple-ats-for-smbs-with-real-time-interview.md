Most ATS are built for big companies with HR teams. Small businesses interview using a Googled question list and a spreadsheet, then decide based on gut feelings. Recrutador is made for them: it helps at every step of the hiring process, including defining evaluation criteria, triaging resumes, and assisting live during interviews.
