Coach Call AI provides an AI accountability partner that keeps you on track with goals through WhatsApp check-ins and scheduled phone calls. It lets you set goals, choose call times, and receive three daily messages that nudge progress. Calls arrive at critical moments for motivation, while conversations automatically log milestones and build a visual timeline of achievements.
