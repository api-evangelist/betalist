FicCrunch builds a bounded reading path and link graph from a single pasted issue so you can navigate cross-repo threads without drowning in tabs. Paste one GitHub issue and get a bounded path of what to open next across repos instead of guessing through many tabs. It orders what to open next and maps how issues, PRs, and discussions connect across repositories.
