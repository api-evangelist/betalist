Spiritual Me is a mindfulness and meditation app that starts with how you feel and guides you through a calming flow of breathing, reflection, and nature-inspired sessions. Skip overwhelming meditation libraries and begin with a simple emotional check-in. Use Spiritual Me privately with no account, track Apple Health mindful minutes, and choose optional spiritual reflections when you want deeper meaning.
