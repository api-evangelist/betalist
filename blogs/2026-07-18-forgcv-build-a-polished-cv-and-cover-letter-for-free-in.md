Creating a CV is often slow, requires accounts, and many tools hide features behind paywalls. ForgCV solves this by offering a completely free and simple CV builder where users can create and download a professional resume instantly without signup. The focus is on speed, simplicity, and accessibility, allowing anyone to generate a CV in seconds.
