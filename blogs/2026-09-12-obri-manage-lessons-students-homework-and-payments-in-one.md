OBRI is tutor management software built for independent tutors, online teachers, and small course creators. It centralizes student tracking, lesson scheduling, homework collection, payment tracking, and reporting in a single workspace. You can sync Google Calendar, send reminders, manage rates and debts, and generate reports for parents and yourself.
