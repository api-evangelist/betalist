Every demo data generator gives you a file, but after a month, the dates get old and you have to rebuild it by hand or regenerate, which usually changes the rest of your data too. As of Now saves the recipe instead: your tables, their fields, and how they relate. Dates are relative to now ("in the last 90 days"), so every generation has the same IDs, names, and amounts, but new dates.
