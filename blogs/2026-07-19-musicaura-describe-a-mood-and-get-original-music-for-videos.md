Musicaura lets creators turn moods, scenes, or lyric ideas into original, creator-ready music for videos, podcasts, games, ads, and social posts. Describe the atmosphere, preview multiple results, then download or refine to match your project. Beyond text-to-music, Musicaura includes an AI lyrics generator, vocal remover, and stem splitter so you can write, separate, and edit audio in the browser.
