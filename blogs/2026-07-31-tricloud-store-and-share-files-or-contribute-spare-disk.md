Tricloud is a Windows desktop cloud-storage beta that lets you store, share, back up, and access files offline while optionally contributing selected unused disk space from your PC to an experimental, centrally coordinated distributed network. It keeps authentication and metadata on a central server and uses no blockchain or tokens. The beta focuses on small, replaceable test files and provides a familiar cloud-drive interface where you can allocate, monitor, and stop a provider node as you test.
