Shoturo is an AI photo editor for car listings that fixes backgrounds and lighting on the photo you already have so buyers see the car, not the driveway. Upload a real listing shot, pick a cleaner scene, refine the light, and export a consistent, listing-ready image in about a minute. Turo hosts, private sellers, indie lots, flippers, and dealerships use it to standardize first photos, improve click-through, and turn more views into bookings.
