Mera is a sports technology platform for VO2max and metabolic performance testing. It combines physiological data from various testing systems into a single athlete profile and uses data science, specialized neural networks, and Mera AI to detect metabolic thresholds, compare tests, and track athlete readiness over time. Mera also develops optical sensing hardware for metabolic testing with faster response times, no routine calibration, and no annual sensor replacement, streamlining workflows for sport performance clubs and athlete testing labs.
