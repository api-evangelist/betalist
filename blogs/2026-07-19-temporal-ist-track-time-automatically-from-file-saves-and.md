Temporal.ist tracks your work automatically by monitoring file saves in designated project folders and matching visited URLs, then builds clear timesheets and summaries for accurate billing. View weekly calendars, per-project hours, and invoice with confidence. It runs in the background without screenshots, keystroke logging, or productivity scores, keeping your data private and editable when needed.
