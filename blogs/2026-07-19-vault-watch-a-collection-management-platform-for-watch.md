Vault Watch is a collection management app for watch collectors, built on a manually verified reference database organized by brand family, variant, and reference. It covers 500+ references across brands like Omega, Audemars Piguet, and Armani Exchange, with new entries added daily and every spec checked by hand rather than crowdsourced. Available as a web app now, with iOS and Android in final beta.
