GolfWiz is an AI-powered golf swing analyzer that offers instant, frame-by-frame feedback and detects over 50 common swing and putting errors. It provides personalized, follow-along drills and includes a smart putting coach to help you practice with purpose and improve faster. Use swing tracer, voice assist, and progress tracking to measure your improvement and train anywhere.
