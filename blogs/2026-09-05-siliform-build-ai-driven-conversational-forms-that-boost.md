SiliForm is an AI-native form builder. Describe your form in plain language and it builds a conversational flow that asks one question at a time and adapts to each answer, with no logic tree to maintain. Every response gets a 0–100 quality score, and an opt-in behavioral layer flags likely AI-written answers.
