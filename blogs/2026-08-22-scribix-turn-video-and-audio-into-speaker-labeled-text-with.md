Scribix converts video and audio into speaker-labeled transcripts you can review and export. Upload MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, MP3, WAV, or M4A files and get word-level timestamps with automatic language detection. You can edit transcripts inline and download them as TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, or CSV files.
