Inbox Ledger is a tool that turns your email into a clean ledger of invoices and receipts. You connect Gmail, Outlook or IMAP, forward a document, or upload a PDF, and it reads the vendor, dates, amounts and line items with AI. No templates, no manual data entry, no folder of PDFs you keep meaning to sort.
