RoboRP lets you chat with AI characters and create private roleplay experiences. You can choose from well-crafted personalities, set the mood and rules, and run group chats with up to ten characters for richer conversations. The platform understands context, remembers past messages, and adapts to your preferences across languages to keep replies natural and engaging.
