ZeroDesk is an AI-native workspace for individuals, teams, and organizations to manage knowledge, files, tasks, workflows, permissions, and intelligent AI agents in one system. It is designed for users who need more than a notes app, file drive, or project management tool, helping people organize work, search information, collaborate, and use AI directly within daily workflows. For organizations, it adds governance, role-based access, workflow automation, and secure knowledge management to turn scattered coordination into real execution. View startup
