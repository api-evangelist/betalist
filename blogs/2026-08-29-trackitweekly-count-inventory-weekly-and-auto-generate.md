TrackItWeekly streamlines weekly inventory for restaurants and multi-location operators. Count from your phone with barcode scanning and offline mode, then get PAR levels from a rolling 3-week average with clear green, yellow, and red guidance. Send vendor-ready order emails in one tap and hold every location accountable with logs and role-based access.
