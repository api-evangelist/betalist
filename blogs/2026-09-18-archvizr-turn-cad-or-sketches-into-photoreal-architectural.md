Upload a SketchUp, Revit, Rhino, CAD export, or even a hand sketch, and get photoreal images in as little as 20 seconds while preserving geometry, camera, and composition. Generate consistent sets across exteriors, interiors, landscapes, and multiple lighting conditions, and apply style references to match materials and mood.
