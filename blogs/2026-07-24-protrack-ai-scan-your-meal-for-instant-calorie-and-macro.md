ProTrack AI is an on-device calorie and macro tracker for iPhone that scans meal photos locally to estimate calories, protein, carbs, and fat without uploading images. You can review and edit results before saving, keeping full control of your data. It also supports barcode scanning, manual logging, daily goals, a Home Screen widget, and 7, 30, and 90 day trends.
