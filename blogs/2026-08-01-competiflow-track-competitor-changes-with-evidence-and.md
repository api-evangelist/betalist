Competiflow monitors competitors' pricing, docs, blogs, and changelogs and delivers structured, evidence-backed changes. It extracts meaningful fields and compares those, cutting noise from cosmetic edits. You get severity-ranked summaries, why it matters, and suggested actions in a quiet digest or via email, Slack, or webhooks.
