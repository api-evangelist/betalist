Backlog.cloud joins your Zoom, Meet, or Teams call and writes up the output. Uploaded recordings, transcripts, and images work too. It knows 26 ceremony types and produces up to 12 artefact types: user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria, edge cases, dependencies, bugs, tasks, epics, spikes, requirements, RAID and decision logs, action items, minutes, BPMN process models, and a living discovery pack.
