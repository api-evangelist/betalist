Spawned is an all-in-one cloud platform to deploy containers, databases, functions, and everything else needed for full-stack apps on real infrastructure you own across AWS, GCP, Azure, or your own clusters. It unifies the developer experience of modern deployment platforms with the robustness and ownership of infrastructure as code. The goal of Spawned is to let anyone ship a Netflix from their own basement.
