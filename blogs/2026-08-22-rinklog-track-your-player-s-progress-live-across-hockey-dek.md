RinkLog helps parents, coaches, and scouts capture every shift and event with one tap and turns them into clear season insights. Build a customizable RinkBuddy board for each player, track goals, shots, faceoffs, hits, and ice time, and log EV, PP, and PK by shift, even offline at the rink. After the game, explore full statistics, heat maps, shot zones, and trends to visualize real progress.
