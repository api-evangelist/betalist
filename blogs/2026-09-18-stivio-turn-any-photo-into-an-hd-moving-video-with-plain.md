Stivio is an online image-to-video generator that turns a single photo and a short motion description into an HD MP4 clip. Upload a product shot, portrait, or old photo, describe the movement and camera behavior in plain English, and create a 3–30 second video in minutes. The service combines multiple AI video models, offers transparent per-second pricing, and provides clean exports, lasting share links, and commercial rights on paid plans while keeping your uploads private by default.
