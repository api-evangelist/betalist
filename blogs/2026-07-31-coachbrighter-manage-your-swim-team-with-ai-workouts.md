CoachBrighter is a modern swim team management platform that automates meet seeding, athlete tracking, and results import so coaches spend less time on spreadsheets. Set up your team in minutes, add athletes, schedule meets, and keep everything organized in one place. The built-in AI assistant creates structured swim workouts from a simple prompt, including phases, sets, intervals, and equipment you can refine by chat.
