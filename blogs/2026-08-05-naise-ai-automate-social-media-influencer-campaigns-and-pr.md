Naise AI is an AI marketing teammate that executes social media, influencer campaigns, PR, market research, and image generation from a single brief. It researches your niche, writes copy, creates visuals, schedules posts, discovers and contacts creators, pitches the press, and reports results across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and LinkedIn. It remembers your brand voice, supports multiple languages and markets, and helps agencies scale consistent output across clients.
