Falconer is a self-updating internal documentation and knowledge system that unifies your company’s docs, tribal knowledge, and codebase into one reliable source of truth. It keeps content organized and in sync as teams ship, so people can find accurate answers fast. Falconer helps engineering-led teams stay in flow by providing long-term memory for the organization and trustworthy context for coding agents. Use it to write better technical docs, reduce repeat questions, and scale knowledge across engineering, product, and go-to-market teams. View startup
