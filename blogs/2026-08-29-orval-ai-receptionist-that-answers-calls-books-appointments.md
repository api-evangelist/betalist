Orval is an AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, and captures leads 24/7. It holds natural, human-like conversations, checks live calendar availability, sends SMS confirmations, and routes complex inquiries to your team with context. Orval works with Outlook, Freshdesk, Zendesk, Calendly, and Jobber.
