Image3D AI is an all-in-one AI 3D model generation platform. Upload a single image and get a downloadable 3D model in seconds, powered by leading engines like Tencent Hunyuan 3D, Meshy, Tripo 3D, and the Default Trellis model. The platform offers a complete 3D workflow including AI texture generation from text or reference images, automatic retopology to low-poly meshes, one-click UV unwrapping, photo-to-3D avatar and figurine generators, and 14 format converters such as GLB, OBJ, FBX to STL, USDZ, MP4, and GIF.
