Allervibe lets you scan barcodes to quickly check packaged foods for allergens. Create an allergen profile, scan, and get results showing matches with your allergens, avoid or unknown status, along with full ingredients and additives. You can also share your allergen profile in restaurants to alert them or print it to carry with you.
