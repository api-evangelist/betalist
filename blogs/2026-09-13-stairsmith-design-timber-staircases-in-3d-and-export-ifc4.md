StairSmith is a browser-based parametric staircase design tool for practitioners. It lets you model straight, single winder, and double winder timber stairs with a live 3D preview. You can set rise, going, width, and shape kite winders, then export IFC4 models for BIM coordination and dimensioned DXF plans, elevations, and sections.
