Geo-SEO-Check offers an automated visibility check for websites, assessing both classic SEO for Google and presence in AI responses. The SEO check analyzes technical aspects, metadata, structure, and indexing, while the GEO check verifies if brands appear in ChatGPT and other AI tools, if AI crawlers are allowed access, and if content is machine-readable. Users get a free teaser with a score and can purchase detailed reports or subscriptions for daily checks.
