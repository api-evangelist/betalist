Discourse AI helps schools turn any topic into a complete, trackable course in minutes. It generates modules, lessons, quizzes, and interactive 3D labs that you can edit and assign, then shows progress at learner and cohort levels. Built for UK schools, it supports SSO, two-factor sign-in, GDPR compliance, 13 languages, and accessible reading modes.
