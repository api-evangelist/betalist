TickKet brings your entire business trip into one iPhone app. Forward any confirmation email and it builds a clean timeline of flights, hotels, cars, and plans. It then monitors every leg with Hugo, an AI concierge that alerts you to gate changes, suggests options near your gate, and answers questions in context.
