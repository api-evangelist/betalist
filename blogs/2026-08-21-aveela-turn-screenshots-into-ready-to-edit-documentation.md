Every client handoff used to involve taking screenshots, writing instructions, arranging images, exporting PDFs, and then repeating it for the next client. Aveela changes that process by capturing a workflow with screenshots and turning them into a structured draft with headings, ordered steps, and images in place. You can review, annotate, rewrite, or translate the draft with AI, and publish a branded documentation portal for each client.
