BirchRx connects Canadians with licensed physicians for prescriptions covering weight management, hair loss, and erectile dysfunction. Complete a short online assessment with no video call; a Canadian doctor reviews it and, if appropriate, a licensed Canadian pharmacy ships your medication discreetly to your door. BirchRx stores data in Canada and complies with PIPEDA.
