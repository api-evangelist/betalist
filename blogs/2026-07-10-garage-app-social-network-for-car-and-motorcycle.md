GarageApp is a social network built for car and motorcycle enthusiasts. Members create a virtual garage to showcase their vehicles, discover local car shows and meetups, and plan scenic driving routes with real-time sharing through CarPlay and Android Auto. The platform makes it easy to find and connect with other owners by make and model, join interest-based groups, and message directly within the app.
