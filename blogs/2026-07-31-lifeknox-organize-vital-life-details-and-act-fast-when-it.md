LifeKnox is a secure, shareable vault for everyday details that can become urgent. Store pet medical records, household info, insurance, vehicle, and work documents, then turn them into clear next steps with guided checklists for accidents, moves, emergencies, and care handoffs. Share only what someone needs with sitters, roommates, or family, and quickly access records when plans change.
