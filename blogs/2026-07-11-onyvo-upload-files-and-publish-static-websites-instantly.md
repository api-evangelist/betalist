Onyvo is a simple static hosting platform for people who want to publish a website without dealing with Git, build pipelines, or complicated tools. You upload your files, and your site is online instantly with no configuration, waiting, or technical hurdles. It has a clean dashboard with drag-and-drop deployment, a visual file manager, inline code editing, WebDAV access, automatic SSL, a global CDN, form handling, analytics, backups, and privacy-friendly EU hosting.
