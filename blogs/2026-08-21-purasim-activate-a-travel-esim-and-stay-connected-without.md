PuraSim offers a travel eSIM to connect you in 218 destinations without paying roaming. You buy online, get a QR code, and activate it in 1 minute; you keep your number and WhatsApp, and you can share data via hotspot. Choose plans by country, region, or worldwide coverage and pay only for the GB and days you need.
