Capturable is an AI cinema studio for planning, generating, and finishing multi-scene videos. It locks approved character references and chains frames so your cast stays consistent across shots. Then you can assemble scenes, voiceover, music, and captions on a multi-track timeline and export a delivery-ready cut.
