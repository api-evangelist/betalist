InvizCrypt offers real-time LaTeX editing with client-side encryption that locks your source before it leaves your browser. It compiles PDFs locally using WebAssembly and supports live collaboration via invite-only links with conflict-free sync. A ciphertext inspector confirms no server access. Keys are derived and stored on your devices, not servers. Work across devices using templates, multi-file projects, citations, and live preview without installs or account limits. View startup
