Got Cosy scores hotels for cosiness based on the warmth, character, and intimacy guests actually feel. Our AI reads guest reviews against a fixed rubric and scores each hotel from 0 to 10, showing the signals it found. Star ratings rarely predict this well, so our lists highlight places usual filters miss.
